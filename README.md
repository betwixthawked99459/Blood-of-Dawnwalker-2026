## 🕹️ About

The Blood of Dawnwalker is an open-world dark fantasy action RPG developed by Rebel Wolves and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Set in 14th-century Europe during the Black Death, it follows Coen, a young man who becomes human by day and a vampire by night. Scheduled for release on September 3, 2026, the game combines narrative choices, supernatural combat, and a reactive world.

Built for players who enjoy story-rich single-player action RPGs, dark fantasy worlds, exploration, and consequential choices.

## ✨ Features

- 🌗 **Day and Night** — Switch between human and vampire gameplay with distinct abilities and approaches.
- ⚔️ **Dual Combat** — Fight with swords and magic as a human, or claws and supernatural powers as a vampire.
- 🗺️ **Open-World Exploration** — Explore forests, plains, swamps, mountains, settlements, ruins, and ancient secrets.
- ⏳ **Time-Driven Story** — Every quest advances time, forcing meaningful choices about who to help.
